ABOUT US

Crevolution is a FIRST® Robotics team composed of students from the Utica Community Schools area. We were founded in 2008 as a sister team to the Thunderchickens, another team in the area, and first competed in the 2009 "Lunacy" season.

Crevolution at Woodhaven Competition 2024

Crevolution competes in competitions in March and April, and we design, build, and program our robot during the Winter after kickoff in January. Although we compete early in the year, we are always building our community, hosting outreach events including STEAM workshops and summer camps, and creating an FTC league for the Macomb County area!

Crevolution's Pinewood Derby 2025 Outreach Event hosted at our build site.

Our team has 9 departments (listed below) with technical and business subteams! We are located at the Gene L. Klida Utica Academy for International Studies (UAIS) on Dodge Park & 16 Mile Rd. If you are interested in joining our team, reach out to crevo.robotics@gmail.com.
